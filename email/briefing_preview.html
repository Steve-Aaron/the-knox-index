Jonathan Hinder hits 35,000 views by weaponizing regional rail rage. It turns out one relatable grievance is worth more than Chris Philp's 22 low-impact posts.

On May 31, 2026, Labour dominated TikTok as Jonathan Hinder MP captured 35,929 views and a massive 9.87x virality rate by weaponizing a highly relatable regional grievance about Northern rail services. UKLabour secured the second spot with 29,224 total views, proving that high-indignation political attacks (27,000 views) drive far more traction than soft, pop-culture trends (2,224 views). Conversely, Chris Philp's high-volume strategy of 22 posts this week yielded just 4,628 total views, demonstrating that sheer output cannot compensate for a lack of sharp, localized emotional hooks.
